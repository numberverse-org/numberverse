# #233 — Science: day & night — earth rotation

**Status:** ✅ done

## Problem
Band-2 (ages 4–6, science) needed a game connecting earth rotation to
day/night: a spin visual, a "why does it happen?" question, i18n clean.

## Fix
New Phaser scene `DayNightScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/DayNightScene.ts`):

- **Spin visual** — the sun sits on the left; the Earth spins continuously and
  a house rides its rim. The house stays upright (position-only tween, not a
  container rotate) so it reads clearly to a 4-year-old. Blue globe, green
  continent, white axis line, day/night terminator.
- **Day/night state rounds (levels 0-1)** — the spin freezes with the house
  on the sunny side (Day) or the dark side (Night) and the kid answers Day/Night.
- **Why-does-it-happen rounds (levels 2-5)** — the Earth keeps spinning calmly
  behind the question ("What makes day and night?" / "turns away from the sun?
  / toward the sun?"). 3 answer options at levels 2-3, 4 at levels 4-5 (extra
  cosmology distractor). The true answer is always "the Earth spins".
- **Difficulty ladder** — `game-daynight` `[2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 4]` = answer-option
  count per tier (2 → 3 → 4).
- **i18n clean** — EN + BN for every question, answer, hint, and speech line.
