# Work Order #243 — Parent: per-kid sound & music balance

**Status:** DONE · **PR:** #557 · **Branch:** feat/243-sound-balance · **Merge:** main

## Problem
The "Reading & sound" block only had on/off toggles (voice, music, voice
recording). Parents couldn't balance the volume of the three sound layers —
a quiet-time family and a noisy room need different mixes per kid.

## Fix
Three independent volume sliders per kid in the portal's Reading & sound block,
persisted per kid and applied by the kid app.

- **3 sliders per kid** — Voice 🗣️ / Sound effects ✨ / Music 🎵, 0–100%, saved
  with the kid's other settings (`PUT /kids/{id}/settings`) and read back by the
  kid app on the same server sync (offline cache respects them too).
- **Try-button** — every slider has 🔊 Try, which previews that layer at the
  current level via pure client-side WebAudio (synthesized, zero assets, offline).
- **Persisted per kid** — three new `Float` columns on `kid_settings`
  (`voice_volume`, `sfx_volume`, `music_volume`, server_default "1" → existing
  rows keep full volume, auto-migrated by migrations.py on boot).

### Kid-app consumption (the "why it matters" half)
- `audio.ts` — `setSfxVolume()`/`setMusicVolume()`; every `tone()` is scaled by
  its layer (SFX feedback vs the music bed), music notes pass the `"music"`
  layer, and the whoosh respects the sfx knob.
- `speech.ts` — `setVoiceBalance()` scales every spoken line (web + neural) on
  top of the kid's own Soft/Normal/Loud preference.
- `App.tsx` — three effects apply the parent's knobs instantly when settings
  change, so a live balance tweak is heard immediately.
