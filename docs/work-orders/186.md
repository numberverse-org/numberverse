# Work Order — #186: English word families (-at -an -ig -op)

**Status:** ✅ Merged via PR #513

## Problem
Band 1 phonics needed the rhyming-family skill — words that share an ending
rhyme and belong together (cat/hat/bat/mat). Sorting real words into family
buckets makes the pattern tactile and self-checking.

## Fix
New `WordFamilyScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/WordFamilyScene.ts`), band 1, scene
key `WordFamily`, skill `en-wordfamilies` (c1-english-4), game `game-wordfam`
(difficultyLevels `[2,2,3,3,3,3]` — 2 buckets early, 3 later).

**Mechanic — sort into rhyming buckets, with feedback (done-when):**
- **8 families × 4 words (done-when):** -at (cat hat bat mat), -an, -ig, -op,
  -og, -ug, -en, -et — 32 words, every word ends with its family suffix, none
  shared across families.
- Each round shows 2 or 3 family buckets (drop zones) and 8 or 12 scrambled
  word tiles. The kid DRAGS a word to its bucket.
- **Sort with feedback (done-when):** a correct drop pops, speaks the word, and
  flies the tile into the bucket as a locked green chip; a wrong drop buzzes
  (camera shake) and bounces the tile back to its grid slot. The round wins the
  moment every tile is placed, then speaks each family aloud.
