# #235 — Social: village & city life sort

**Status:** ✅ done

## Problem
Band-1/2 social-studies (BGS) needed a sorting game for village vs city scenes:
scene cards with Bangla+EN labels, i18n clean.

## Fix
New Phaser scene `VillageCityScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/VillageCityScene.ts`):

- **Scene cards** — every card shows an emoji + BANGLA + ENGLISH label, so
  pre-readers sort by icon and older kids learn both words. 6 easy cards
  (cow/paddy/chicken = village; building/bus/signal = city) + 6 tricky cards
  that join at level 2 (frog/mango/tree; hospital/shop/train).
- **Two zones** — the village 🏡 (green, left) and the city 🏙️ (blue, right)
  fill the bottom band. The kid drags each card into its zone.
- **Gentle sorting** — wrong or mid-air drops float "Try again!" and the card
  glides home (no harsh fail); correct drops pop a sparkle and fly into the zone.
- **Cards per round** 4 → 6 → 8 (levels 0-1 → 2-3 → 4-5); the tricky pool only
  appears from level 2.
- **Mixed-deal guard** — a deal is re-drawn (bounded) if it would be all one
  side, so every round has both a village and a city card to sort.
- **i18n clean** — EN + BN for title, zones, prompt, and cheers.
