# Work Order 209 — Science: water cycle for kids

**Issue:** #209
**Status:** DONE (PR #536)

## Problem
"Evaporation → condensation → rain" is abstract until you SEE water turn to
vapor, rise into a cloud, and rain back down. A kid needs the loop animated,
not recited.

## Fix
New `WaterCycleScene` — a band-2 ordering game over the 4-step water cycle:
- **Order the steps:** the kid taps the shuffled step cards in the right
  order — **Water → Vapor → Cloud → Rain**. Lvl 1 sequences just
  water→vapor→cloud (3 steps); lvl 2+ adds rain (4 steps).
- **Kid cartoon scene:** a smiling sun with 8 drawn rays, a grassy bank around
  a blue pond, and a puffy white cloud. Every correct tap animates that step:
  Water → ✨ sparkles over the pond; Vapor → 💨 steam wisps rise from the pond
  to the sky; Cloud → the cloud scales in with a back-elastic pop; Rain → 💧
  raindrops fall from the cloud into the pond.
- **Timeline strip** of the step emojis lights up as each step is placed, and
  the floating "Water cycle!" toast + a spoken line ("Water collects in the
  pond", "The sun turns water into vapor"…) narrates the completed step.
- **Speaks** the target on every prompt ("Tap the vapor") in EN or BN.
- **Wrong taps** shake the card, flash the correct prompt, and re-speak it.

Wired as skill `en-water-cycle` (band 2 science, c2-science-10, class 2), game
`game-watercycle`, scene key `WaterCycle`, bg "sky". i18n: 13 new wc* keys
(EN + BN).
