# Work Order 189 — Math: subtraction facts to 20 fluency

**Issue:** #189
**Status:** DONE (PR #516)

## Problem
Band 1/2 kids need the subtraction twin of the addition sprint (#188): a timed,
abstract recall drill for subtraction facts to 20 — but with a visual number-line
aid so "minus" means jumping backward on a line, not just digits.

## Fix
New `SubFactsScene` — a rapid-fire minus sprint with a persistent 0–20 number line:
- **Timed:** each fact shows "a − b = ?" with a shrinking 6s countdown bar
  (timeout records a wrong answer + "Time's up!").
- **Visual number-line aid:** a 0–20 strip is always visible at the bottom with
  tick marks (labeled every 5). The minuend `a` is marked yellow at question
  time; after answering, a green ball hops backward `b` steps from `a` and lands
  on the difference, then the marker sits on the answer (= shown).
- **Levelled:** minuend range grows — to 10 (lvl 1–2), to 16 (lvl 3), to the
  full 20 family (lvl 4+). Rounds per level: 6/8/10/10/12/12.
- **Speed + accuracy:** per-fact ms, ⚡ under 2.5s, live correct/total tally,
  finish line "Sprint done! 8 correct 1.9s avg ⚡⚡⚡".

Wired as skill `en-subfacts` (band 2 math, c2-math-7, class 2), game
`game-subfacts`, scene key `SubFacts`, bg "ocean". i18n: one new title key
(titleSubFacts EN "Minus Sprint" / BN "বিয়োগ স্প্রিন্ট") reusing the add
sprint's fgSlow/fgDone/fgCorr/fgAvg. Bangla digits for the BN locale.
