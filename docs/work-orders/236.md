# #236 — Values: patience & waiting game

**Status:** ✅ done

## Problem
Band-1 values education needed a patience game with turn-taking and waiting
scenarios: 5+ scenarios, gentle rewards, i18n clean.

## Fix
New Phaser scene `PatienceScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/PatienceScene.ts`):

- **6 waiting scenarios** (5+ required) — grow 🌱→🌸, brew 🍵→☕, cross 🚦→🚌,
  fill 🎈→🎉, hatch 🥚→🐣, and a **turn-taking** swing 🛝→🧒. Every round the
  scene breathes while a progress bar fills.
- **The wait** — the bar fills over `[3,3,4,4,5,5]` seconds by level. No timer
  pressure, no fail state.
- **Temptation** — from level 2 a "Tap me!" bubble pops up; from level 4 a
  second one. Tapping it is never punished — a gentle "not yet, be patient!"
  and the wait continues.
- **Gentle rewards** — when the bar completes, the reward pops in with a
  sparkle burst + cheer ("Patience pays off!"); every level end earns a star.
- **i18n clean** — EN + BN for every waiting line, reward line, and message.
