## Goal
Hasan reported the Settings / parent-facing area was still weak: the full
Parent Portal was buried (only a tiny ghost button → Parent panel → a card
that vanished for guests), the Settings cards were flat/inconsistent (one
even showed a broken "Language … Music" header), and the Bangla used a hard
literary word — "অন্তর্দৃষ্টি" — instead of everyday speech.

## What shipped
**Parent Portal door in Settings** (`apps/kid/src/App.tsx`):
- New `settings-portal-card` at the TOP of Settings, always visible:
  - signed in → `<a>` → `/parent/parent` (same tab, PWA-safe)
  - guest → button → opens the sign-in gate with `gateReturn.current = "parent"`
- New i18n: `settingsPrefs`, `settingsAccount`, `settingsPortalGuest`.

**Parent panel portal card always visible**:
- Signed in → deep-link card (as before). Guest → the same card rendered as
  a `<button>` (`.parent-portal-guest`) that opens the gate instead of hiding.

**Cleaner Settings layout**:
- Portal card first (accent border), then preferences card with a proper
  header "⚙️ Language & sound" + live sync status (fixes the broken
  "Language … Music" parent-head), then voice / syllabus / account /
  Kotha / about — each with its own clean header.
- `styles.css`: `.settings-portal-card`, `.settings-portal-btn`,
  `.settings-parent-btn`, `.parent-portal-guest`.

**Natural Bangla** (every parent-facing string; the words people say):
- aggTitle "🔭 ক্লাসের খবর" · aggOptIn "নাম ছাড়া শেখার খবর ভাগ করুন" ·
  aggOptOut "বন্ধ করুন" (was অপ্ট-আউট) · aggTopMis/aggTopSkills/aggNoneYet naturalized
- অভিভাবক → মা-বাবা (পোর্টাল, প্যানেল, জায়গা, অ্যাকাউন্ট, সাইন-ইন CTA)
- অগ্রগতি → শেখার খবর (sync, guest, sign-in, share)
- শিশু → বাচ্চা · বেনামি → নাম ছাড়া · অন্তর্ভুক্ত/পরিচালনা/নির্ধারণ removed

## Notes
- The full portal (`/parent/parent`, Next.js static export) is reachable and
  its API works — verified `auth/login`, `auth/me`, `kids`, `auth/sessions`
  all 200 on the live tunnel. The gap was UX: no direct door from Settings.
- `nv_parent_token` is written by the kid app gate and read by the portal on
  the same origin — the same-tab deep link carries the session straight over.
