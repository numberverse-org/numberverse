# Work Order #224 — English: long-vowel teams review

**Status:** DONE · **PR:** #551 · **Branch:** feat/224-long-vowel-teams · **Merge:** main

## Problem
Band-2 phonics gap: learners master single letters but not vowel-team
spellings (ai/ay/ee/ea/oa/ow/ie/igh). Issue asks for a speed + accuracy review
consolidating long-vowel teams across word families, mixed teams, i18n clean.

## Fix
New `LongVowelScene` — "Vowel teams!": a picture + word with the vowel team
blanked ("r____n" 🌧️) is spoken aloud; the kid taps the vowel team that makes
the word from 3 options.

- **8 vowel teams × 5 words = 40 words** — ai (rain, tail, mail, train, snail),
  ay (day, play, say, tray, stay), ee (tree, feet, bee, see, green), ea (sea,
  tea, leaf, team, read), oa (boat, goat, coat, road, soap), ow (snow, crow,
  slow, grow, bow), ie (pie, tie, lie, cried, fried), igh (night, light, high,
  right, fight).
- **Mixed teams** — every round draws one word per team (all 8 guaranteed),
  shuffled order; per-team shuffle-bags guarantee all 5 words of each team are
  eventually covered (300k-round sim: 40/40 seen).
- **Speed + accuracy** — shrinking timer bar ramps 10s→5s per level; timeout
  = gentle "Faster!", never a penalty.
- **3 string-distinct team options** — correct team exactly once.
- Ladder `[10000,9000,8000,7000,6000,5000]` ms, `ROUNDS_TO_FINISH = 3`,
  bg "sky".
