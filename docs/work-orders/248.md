# Work Order #248 — Parent: quiet hours scheduling

**Status:** DONE · **PR:** #562 · **Branch:** feat/248-quiet-hours · **Merge:** main

## Problem
No way for a parent to soften the app at bedtime/quiet times — play stayed at
full volume and full brightness no matter the hour.

## Fix
Parent-set **quiet hours** that AUTO-SOFTEN the app during a per-day schedule —
never hard-blocks play.

- **Per-day schedule** — a "🌙 Quiet hours" editor in the portal: 7 rows
  (Mon..Sun), each with an On checkbox + start/end time pickers. Stored as
  `quiet_windows` = 7 comma-joined `"HH:MM-HH:MM"` (`-` = off that day); a
  window that starts LATER than it ends means overnight (e.g. `20:00-07:00`).
  Mirrors the existing `weekday_limits_min` pattern exactly.
- **Gentle dimming** — during a quiet window the kid app:
  · auto-lowers EVERY sound (voice + sfx + music) to the parent's "soft volume"
    knob via a new `quietLevel` multiplier layered on top of the #243 balance
    (both the WebAudio engine and the speech layer);
  · shows a soft radial dim over the screen (pointer-transparent — play is
    unaffected) plus a fixed 🌙 badge.
  A minute tick re-checks, so overnight/midnight boundaries stay correct.
- **Parent override** — the master "Quiet hours on" toggle + soft-volume slider
  + dim toggle are in the portal; the kid can also tap the 🌙 badge to restore
  full sound for the rest of the session (a kindness, never a bypass — the
  schedule stays parent-controlled). Wrong: never hard-blocks.
- **i18n-ready** — all new strings + the badge in EN + বাংলা (country-pack seam
  unchanged); `quietTimeNow()` is a pure shared helper mirroring the editor.
