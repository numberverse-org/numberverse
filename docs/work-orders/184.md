# Work Order — #184: English read-aloud comprehension lvl 2

**Status:** ✅ Merged via PR #___

## Problem
Band 2 reading needed a comprehension step up: not just decoding words, but
understanding a full sentence and answering a question about it. Reading a
short sentence aloud + tapping the picture that answers the question makes
comprehension concrete and self-checking.

## Fix
New `ReadAloudScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/ReadAloudScene.ts`), band 2, scene
key `ReadAloud`, skill `en-readaloud` (c2-english-10), game `game-readaloud`
(difficultyLevels `[3,3,3,4,4,4]` — 3 options early, 4 later).

**Mechanic — read, then tap the picture (done-when):**
- **18 sentences (done-when, ≥15):** all 5-7 words ("The cat is on the mat.",
  "The bird can fly up high.", "We make a snowman in winter." …). Each has a
  picture-answer question + 3 distractor pictures (3-4 options in the bank).
  Distractors test the same subject in the wrong place, the wrong color, or the
  wrong subject — and every caption is unique in-bank.
- **Read-aloud toggle (done-when):** a 🔊/🔇 button. When ON the sentence is
  auto-spoken each round and the button re-reads it on tap; when OFF the voice
  is muted and the button shows 🔇.
- Correct tap pops + re-reads the sentence; wrong tap → `hintWrong` dims the
  tile ("Read the sentence again — which picture fits?").
