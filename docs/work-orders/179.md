# Work Order — #179: English comparatives & superlatives (big-bigger-biggest)

**Status:** ✅ Merged via PR #___

## Problem
Band 3 grammar needed the degrees of comparison: positive → comparative →
superlative (big → bigger → biggest). Kids know the words but not the RULE (two
things → -er, three things → -est).

## Fix
New `CompareScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/CompareScene.ts`), band 3, scene key
`Compare`, skill `en-compare` (c3-english-5), game `game-compare`
(difficultyLevels `[3,3,3,3,3,3]`).

**Mechanic — rules demo + picture compare (done-when):**
- **Rules taught with demo (done-when):** a rule strip of three degree chips
  (big → bigger → biggest) sits at the top; whenever the adjective changes the
  chips pulse and SPEAK in order, teaching the -er/-est pattern before asking.
  5 size adjectives (big/small/tall/short/long) × 8 picture items (🐕🐈🌳🐟🚌✏️🐍🪱).
- **Picture compare (done-when):** two pictures (small vs big, scaled) →
  "The {noun} on the right is ___ than the one on the left!" → comparative;
  three pictures (small/medium/large) → "The {noun} on the right is the ___!"
  → superlative. Options are always the 3 degree forms (shuffled). Levels 1-2
  teach comparative only; later levels alternate in the superlative.
- Correct → the matching rule-strip chip pulses gold + the degree word is spoken.
  Wrong → that card retires and the correct form is re-spoken.
