# Work Order #239 — Parent: struggle-alert card (skill needs help)

**Status:** DONE · **PR:** #555 · **Branch:** feat/239-struggle-alert · **Merge:** main

## Problem
Parents only see numbers (mastery %, correct/attempts) — nothing tells them
*which skills Moni has flagged* as a real struggle (a repeating misconception,
tiredness, a gap), or what to do about it.

## Fix
New "🚨 Needs a little help" card on the parent dashboard (right after the
weekly narrative) that lists the **top 3 Moni-flagged skills** from the last
7 days with a plain-language reason and a **reteach hook**.

- **Top 3 flagged skills** — re-derives the flags client-side from the kid's
  already-fetched Kotha stories (the synced form of the kid app's local
  wrong-answer ledger): misconception (×5) > fatigue (×4) > gap/guessing (×2)
  > plain wrongs. Each row names the skill (EN + BN titles) and why it's
  flagged, with the specific misconception named where Moni fingerprinted one
  (e.g. "mixing up tens and ones").
- **Links to reteach** — every flagged skill gets a 🔄 Reteach QR: a
  client-side `NV:<skillId>` QR the kid scans in the app to jump straight into
  that skill's game (same pattern as the flashcard QR cards). No network.
- **Uses local ledger** — the flags are computed purely from the stories the
  portal already holds (the ledger's synced mirror); nothing new is fetched or
  sent. The reason text follows the narrative card's EN/BN language toggle.
