## What shipped

A rolling **12-month archive** of monthly learning summaries parents can browse
— generated **client-side** (offline-generated) and **exportable** as CSV.

### Backend (`apps/api`)
- `GET /kids/{kid_id}/activity` limit cap **200 → 2000** — one fetch now covers
  a full year of rounds for the archive (additive; default stays 50).

### Parent portal (`apps/parent`)
- **`buildMonthlyArchive(activity)`** — pure, buckets the activity feed into the
  last 12 calendar months (zero-filled so an idle month shows honestly): rounds,
  correct %, minutes (Σ `response_ms`), top-3 skills by play. Month-name map is
  EN + বাংলা (the #131 country-pack seam).
- **"🗓️ Monthly archive" card** — 12-month grid (tap a month to expand: rounds /
  % correct / minutes + top skills), **⬇️ Export CSV** (full 12-month archive via
  a client-side blob — nothing sent to a server).
- **Lazy load** — the full history (up to 2000 rounds) is fetched once per kid
  and cached in state; the summaries are then derived offline. A `storage`/
  loaded-state guard keeps the dashboard snappy on multi-kid accounts.

## i18n
- Month names ship EN + BN; the archive is generated in the parent's language
  toggle (`narrLang`). The month-name map is a config map — new locales = new
  keys, no rewrites.
