# Work Order — #187: English yes/no listening comprehension

**Status:** ✅ Merged via PR #514

## Problem
Band 1 listening needed comprehension: hear a sentence, hear a question about
it, decide if the answer is yes or no. A simple ✅/❌ tap keeps it to a single
decision while building real listening stamina.

## Fix
New `YesNoScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/YesNoScene.ts`), band 1, scene key
`YesNo`, skill `en-yesno` (c1-english-5), game `game-yesno`
(difficultyLevels `[0,0,0,0,0,0]` — semantic variety, every item is a single
yes/no decision).

**Mechanic — hear, decide, tap (done-when):**
- **22 questions (done-when, ≥20):** statement + spoken question (12 yes / 10
  no, balanced). "The sky is blue. Is the sky blue?" → YES; "Cats can fly. Can
  cats fly?" → NO. All answers are unambiguous for a band-1 kid.
- The statement and question are shown as text AND spoken in one line. The kid
  taps ✅ YES (green) or ❌ NO (red).
- **Slow-replay button (done-when):** a 🐢 button re-speaks the whole line at
  rate 0.7 for kids who need to hear it more slowly.
- Correct tap pops + speaks a confirmation ("Yes, the sky is blue!"). Wrong tap
  → `hintWrong` dims the tapped button and reveals the answer.
