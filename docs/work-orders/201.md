# Work Order 201 — Math: perimeter intro (measure the fence)

**Issue:** #201
**Status:** DONE (PR #528)

## Problem
Band 3 kids need a first meeting with perimeter that they can COUNT before they
formula-ise: a fence is the sum of the side lengths around a shape, and unit
cubes along each edge make "how long is the fence?" a measurable thing.

## Fix
New `PerimFenceScene` — add the side lengths around a fence:
- **2–4 sides:** rectangles (4 sides, lvl 1–2) and right triangles (3 sides,
  lvl 3+) with integer hypotenuses from Pythagorean triples (3-4-5 … 12-16-20).
- **Unit-cubes aid:** a unit-cube "fence post" is drawn along every straight
  edge (all 4 rectangle edges; both triangle legs) so the kid can count each
  side. Sides are also labelled with their lengths.
- **Question:** "How long is the fence?" → the kid adds the labelled sides and
  taps the perimeter from 4 cards (e.g. 24 units). Correct → "The fence is 24
  units long" is spoken (peSpeak).
- Distractors are near-perimeters (P±…); the correct total never repeats.

Wired as skill `en-perim` (band 3 math, c3-math-13, class 3), game
`game-perim`, scene key `PerimFence`, bg "forest". i18n: 6 new pe* keys
(EN + BN). Bangla digits for the BN locale.
