# Work Order 195 — Math: division as sharing lvl 2

**Issue:** #195
**Status:** DONE (PR #522)

## Problem
Band 3 kids already know even fair shares (level 1). Level 2 extends sharing to
the REMAINDER concept: 14 ÷ 4 = 3 each, 2 left over — taught visually.

## Fix
New `ShareRemScene` — candy-sharing game:
- **Sharing animation:** a pile of total candies (up to 24, grid-arranged) is
  dealt ROUND-ROBIN into k kid plates (each round flies k candies in parallel,
  ≤4 rounds) until no full round remains.
- **Remainder concept:** the leftover candies (r, always 1..k−1) stay in the
  pile and are highlighted (bounce + turn green) with a "Left over!" float and
  spoken call-out.
- **Two-step answer:** after watching, the kid answers "Each kid gets __"
  (quotient) then "Left over __" (remainder). Correct → "14 ÷ 4 = 3 R 2" floats
  up and is spoken (Bangla for BN).
- Generation: kids 2–5, each-gets 2–3/4 by level, remainder 1..kids−1 (never a
  clean split — this game is about the remainder).

Wired as skill `en-shares2` (band 3 math, c3-math-7, class 3), game
`game-shares2`, scene key `ShareRem`, bg "candy". i18n: 7 new sh* keys
(EN + BN) — sr* was taken by the story scene, hence sh*. Bangla digits for BN.
