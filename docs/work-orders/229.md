# #229 — Rounding to nearest 10/100 with number-line jumps

**Status:** ✅ done

## Problem
Band-3 (ages 6–8) needed a rounding game: round numbers to the nearest ten or
hundred with a visual number-line jump, i18n clean.

## Fix
New Phaser scene `RoundingScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/RoundingScene.ts`):

- **Number-line jump visual** — the number sits between its two fence-posts on a
  real line (e.g. `40 · 47 · 50`). The two answer buttons ARE the fence-posts at
  the line's endpoints. Tap the nearer one and the marker ball JUMPS onto it.
- **Nearest 10 + 100** — levels 0–2 round to the nearest ten (numbers up to
  120), levels 3–5 to the nearest hundred (up to 999). Fence-posts and
  half-way ties are excluded so every round has ONE clearly nearer answer.
- **Localized** — header title, 3 rotating prompt phrasings per mode (framing
  #337), speech line, buttons and hint digits all EN + BN (Bangla digits in bn).
- Registered in engine + core catalog (`round-nearest-10-100`, band 3 math,
  3.NBT.1) + seed parity + audit scope cap.
