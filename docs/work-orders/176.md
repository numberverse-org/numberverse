# Work Order — #176: English silent-e magic (make-a-word)

**Status:** ✅ Merged via PR #___

## Problem
Band 2 English needed the silent-e rule: the magic-e that flips a short vowel
long (cap → cape) — the single most famous phonics transformation, and the
prerequisite for decoding half of English's long-vowel words.

## Fix
New `MagicEScene` (`apps/kid/src/game/MagicEScene.ts`), band 2, scene key
`MagicE`, skill `en-magic-e` (c2-english-5), game `game-magice`
(difficultyLevels `[3,3,3,4,4,4]` = option count).

**Mechanic — make-a-word with a visual magic-e reveal (done-when):**
- **16 transformations** (cap→cape, tap→tape, kit→kite, pin→pine, man→mane,
  not→note, hop→hope, rob→robe, cub→cube, tub→tube, cut→cute, bit→bite,
  dim→dime, fin→fine, rid→ride, can→cane), each with a picture clue and the
  word pair spoken aloud ("cap… cape").
- The CVC word sits in a word box next to a dashed magic slot; the kid taps the
  magic letter among 3-4 vowels. A correct tap FUSES the word — "cap" + "e" →
  "cape", the e glows gold, the slot pops bigger, the picture pops, and the long
  word is spoken (visual magic-e reveal). Wrong letters shake the slot, retire
  that tile, and re-speak the pair.
