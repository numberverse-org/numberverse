# Work Order 188 — Math: addition facts to 20 fluency

**Issue:** #188
**Status:** DONE (PR #515)

## Problem
Band 1/2 kids who already know "add within 20" need a fluent, timed recall drill —
not another concrete apples game. The goal was instant recall of addition facts
to 20 with speed + accuracy tracking.

## Fix
New `FactsAddScene` — a rapid-fire math sprint:
- **Timed:** each fact shows "a + b = ?" with a shrinking 6s countdown bar
  (timeout is recorded as a wrong answer + "Time's up!").
- **Levelled:** fact range grows by level — sums to 10 (lvl 1–2), to 16 (lvl 3),
  to the full 20 family (lvl 4+). Rounds per level: 6/8/10/10/12/12 facts.
- **Speed tracking:** per-fact ms measured; anything under 2.5s earns a ⚡.
- **Accuracy:** live correct/total tally; on finish shows "Sprint done! 8 correct
  1.9s avg ⚡⚡⚡".
- **Feedback:** wrong tap → shake + dim + green reveal of the correct sum.

Wired as skill `en-addfacts` (band 2 math, c2-math-6, class 2), game
`game-addfacts`, scene key `FactsAdd`, bg "ocean". i18n keys added in EN + BN
(titleFacts20, fgSlow, fgDone, fgCorr, fgAvg) — Bangla digits shown for the BN
locale.
