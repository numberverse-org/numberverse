# #286 — Star-of-the-week recap card

**Status:** ✅ done (PR pending → issue CLOSE)

## Problem

No kid-facing "this was my week" moment. Acceptance: **Weekly summary** ·
**Spoken option** · **Offline-generated**.

## Fix

A **🌟 Star of the Week** card on home (just under the mastery box) that
celebrates the trailing 7 days, derived PURELY offline from local play:

- **Days played this week** — `NovaState.days[]` filtered to today-back-6.
- **Top skill** — the game skill most practiced this week (attempts, among
  skills whose `lastPractice` falls inside the 7-day window).
- **Biggest win** — new masteries this week (`pMastered ≥ threshold` +
  practiced in-window), else the standing streak (≥3) as the win line.
- **Spoken option** — a 🔊 "Hear my week" button reads the whole recap aloud
  (Web Speech or neural, whichever is on).
- **Soft nudge** — when the week is empty the card stays gentle ("come
  play!"), never a lecture.

Locale-extensible (#131): all 9 `Strings` entries are EN + BN packed (Bangla
digit via `bnNum`), and the top-skill title reads the locale `titleBn`.
