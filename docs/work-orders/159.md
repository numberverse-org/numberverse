# Work Order #159 — Bangla: two-letter sight word wall (দেখে বলো)

**Status:** ✅ Merged via PR #486

## Problem
Band-1/2 readers (ages 4–6) meet high-frequency Bangla words constantly
(আমি, তুমি, সে, না…) but can't decode them letter-by-letter yet. They need
**whole-word recall** — seeing the word and instantly knowing it — built the
way a classroom word wall does it: one word POPS, the child says it, hears it,
moves on. There was no dedicated Bangla sight-word drill (the existing
`game-sightwords` is the English band-4 game).

## Fix
New `BnSightWordsScene` (scene key `BnSightWords`, skill `bn-sightwords`,
game `game-bnsightwords`, band 2):

- **48 short high-frequency Bangla sight words** (the 26 most common form the
  easy pool for levels 0–1; the full 48 for levels 2+). All 1–2 syllable words
  kids meet in books & conversation.
- **Word wall tour:** a round deals a wall of words; one word at a time POPS
  (yellow ring + bounce + warm spoken Bangla via `speak(word, {lang:"bn"})`).
  The child taps it to hear it read aloud again — look-and-say, not decoding.
- **Difficulty ladder** `[6,6,8,8,9,9]` (wall size). Levels 2+ also shrink the
  pop-highlight before it fades (1200ms → 650ms) so the child must find the word
  by whole-word memory, and add a soft countdown (7s → 5.5s) that gently SKIPS a
  word on timeout — streak resets, never a harsh fail.
- **Gentle wrong-tap feedback** via `hintWrong` (escalating nudge → coaching →
  reveal, bilingual) with the wall cards as options. Correct taps fire
  `correctButton` (combo fireworks) + the word is re-spoken.
- Streak display `Streak: {n} 🔥` for the "speed-reading" motivation.
