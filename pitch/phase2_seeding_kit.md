## Kindergarten pilot — 4-step runbook
1. **Pick 1 pilot** (one kindergarten/play-group the parent knows, ideally English+Bangla medium).
2. **Ask for a 10-min slot.** Show the app on one phone: home → a math game → a Bangla letters game.
   Emphasize: offline (works without internet), no ads, no accounts, no data.
3. **Give them the URL** (not a Play Store link). Two options for kids:
   - Each classroom phone/bookmark opens the PWA and uses "Add to Home Screen",
   - or a teacher phone with the progress share card → parents see the kid's wins on WhatsApp.
4. **Collect feedback in 1 week** via the parent's WhatsApp. Track below.
