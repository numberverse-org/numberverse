---

## 1. THE STRATEGY IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Numberverse already fits schools better than it fits a solo parent funnel:
**offline-first** (BD classrooms have patchy connectivity) · **privacy-first, zero ads** (schools answer to parent councils — this is a selling point, not a constraint) · **Bangla + English in one app** (rare, and exactly what BD private/English-medium schools and EdTech platforms need) · **adaptive mastery + per-kid analytics** (the parent panel is already half a teacher dashboard).

Sequencing: **(1) white-label to an EdTech platform first** (fastest check, they own payments + trust + distribution) → **(2) direct annual school licenses** (recurring base) → **(3) keep the parent app live as the B2B showroom.**

> Already built in this repo: `apps/api` (sync + analytics), `apps/parent` (per-kid dashboard), `apps/admin` (admin surface). A **teacher/classroom view** (see a whole class, assign lessons) is a thin layer on these — it is the single thing that turns "a kid's app" into "software a school buys." Put it on the roadmap ahead of any school signing push.
